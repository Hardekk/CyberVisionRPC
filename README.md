# CyberVision RPC

Discord Rich Presence for the **CyberVision** Cyberpunk 2077 modlist. Available in English and French editions.

## What it shows

- Current quest and objective, or the district you're roaming
- Level, Street Cred, in-game time and weather
- Life path and playtime
- Dark Future survival needs (food, water, energy, nerve)
- NCPD wanted level, from the first star
- Combat (health and weapon)
- Driving and radio activity (optional)
- Braindance and Photo Mode
- Main menu, pause, loading and death screens
